
**📊 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Insight Analysis

📌 Project Objective**

Analyze e-commerce sales and customer behavior using advanced Excel analytics to identify revenue drivers, customer segments, discount effectiveness, and actionable insights that support data-driven business decisions.

**📂 Dataset Overview**

Transaction-level e-commerce sales data
500 orders

**Key fields:**

1) Order Date

2) Customer ID

3) Order ID

4) Product Category

5) Revenue

6) Discount
**
📈 Key KPIs**

* Total Revenue

* Total Orders

* Total Customers

* Average Order Value (AOV)

* Average Discount

* Revenue per Customer

These KPIs provide a high-level view of business performance and customer value.

**🧠 Business Logic & Customer Segmentation**

* To enable deeper analysis, the following logic flags were created:

* High-Value Order – Orders exceeding a defined revenue threshold

* Discounted Order – Orders with applied discounts

* Repeat Customer – Customers with more than one purchase

* High-Discount Order – Orders with discounts above a defined threshold

* VIP Customer – Repeat customers with above-average total spend

This segmentation enabled targeted analysis of revenue concentration and customer behavior.

**🔍 Analytical Techniques Used**

* Pivot tables for aggregation and comparison

* Revenue distribution analysis (histogram logic)

* Outlier detection using quartiles & IQR

* Monthly trend analysis

* Relationship analysis using scatter plots

* Advanced Excel formulas and conditional formatting

**📊 Key Visualizations**

* Revenue Distribution (Histogram)

* Revenue Outlier Analysis (Box-plot logic)

* Discount vs Revenue (Scatter Plot)

* Revenue by Category (Bar Chart)

* Discounted vs Non-Discounted Orders (Donut Chart)

* Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

* Revenue vs Order Volume (Combo Chart)

**💡 Key Business Insights**

1) Revenue is right-skewed, with a small number of high-value orders contributing disproportionately to total revenue.

2) Sales are concentrated in a limited number of product categories, indicating a Pareto-like revenue distribution.

3) Higher discounts do not consistently drive higher revenue, highlighting discount inefficiency.

4) VIP and repeat customers contribute significantly more revenue than one-time buyers.

5) Monthly revenue shows seasonality and fluctuation rather than linear growth.

**🎯 Business Recommendations**

> Prioritize retention and loyalty strategies for VIP and repeat customers to maximize lifetime value.

> Reduce blanket discounting and move toward targeted, data-driven promotions.

> Invest more in top-performing categories while reviewing underperforming ones.

> Increase Average Order Value (AOV) through bundling, cross-selling, and upselling strategies.
